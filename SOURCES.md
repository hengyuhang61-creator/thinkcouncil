# Method sources and scope

- **Compare alternatives** adapts a historical case treated by James R. Hansen in [NASA-TM-111236, Enchanted Rendezvous (1995)](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19960014824). The monograph documents John C. Houbolt's advocacy for lunar-orbit rendezvous and the tradeoffs among mission modes. The worksheet asks for alternatives, costs and reversibility. It does not attribute those exact questions to Houbolt.
- **Invite a counterexample** draws on Francis Bacon's [Novum Organum, Book I, aphorism 46](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Novum_Organum/Book_I_(Spedding)), Spedding edition. The aphorism addresses favoring observations that support a settled view. The worksheet operationalizes this by asking which observation would reverse a decision. No translation has been redistributed.
- **Trace the claim** adapts a local research worksheet, MC-02, originally developed in a personal study of public entrepreneurship materials. Its transferable procedure records claim, evidence basis, date and invalidation conditions. Effectiveness remains an untested hypothesis. This worksheet is a project-authored method definition and does not attribute the procedure to Elon Musk or assert that he uses it.

The MachAtlas example uses [CI evidence](https://github.com/hengyuhang61-creator/machatlas/actions/runs/45278190764), [a downstream case comparison](https://hengyuhang61-creator.github.io/machatlas/cone.html) and [release scope](https://github.com/hengyuhang61-creator/machatlas/blob/v0.1.0/README.md). The potential effect on readership is an explicit hypothesis.

The source filter accepts contributor-declared categories. A source record may still be mistaken and a derived result may depend on assumptions. The rules worksheet shows opposing support and applies a deterministic review rule. It does not inspect citations, score credibility, or substitute for review by a qualified person. The local person catalog and research notes are outside this release. The project software is MIT licensed. The cover and GIF were captured from this project.

The model-assisted hearing also adapts [USGS Structured Decision Making](https://www.usgs.gov/centers/eesc/science/structured-decision-making) for competing objectives and [RAND Robust Decision Making](https://www.rand.org/pubs/research_briefs/RB9701.html) for decisions across plausible futures. The procedures are short editorial adaptations. The model receives the procedure text and can cite evidence IDs from the docket. The application resolves method links from its own catalog. Source verification remains a human step.

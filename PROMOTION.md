# ThinkCouncil launch material

## English project introduction

**For project leads and reviewers: expose what would overturn a decision before committing to it.** Choose a recorded hearing, read the recommendation and open the cross-examination. Follow an evidence ID into the original submitted claim and look at the evidence the system asks you to obtain.

The model selects three procedures from five sourced methods. The second stage challenges actual statements from the first, and the third stage produces a recommendation with dissent, conditions and evidence IDs. Custom hearings run locally. The public cases document examples, outcomes and known errors, including errors that survive valid evidence IDs.

[Public hearings](https://hengyuhang61-creator.github.io/thinkcouncil/) · [Local setup and source](https://github.com/hengyuhang61-creator/thinkcouncil) · [Evaluation](eval/)

## 中文项目介绍

**面向项目负责人和评审者：在承诺之前，先找出什么证据会让你改主意。** 选择一条公开审议回放，读综合建议、展开交叉质询、点证据编号回看提交材料，并查看还需要补什么证据。

模型从五个有出处的方法中选取三种。第二阶段质询第一阶段的判断，第三阶段综合建议、反对意见和重审条件。自定义问题由本机模型处理。公开评测保留模型错误，包括证据编号正确但推断超过材料范围的情况。

[公开回放](https://hengyuhang61-creator.github.io/thinkcouncil/) · [本机运行与源码](https://github.com/hengyuhang61-creator/thinkcouncil) · [评测](eval/)

## Third-party community draft

**Draft.** Before you commit a team to a difficult decision, test the strongest objection. ThinkCouncil stages a three-method hearing, records what evidence is missing, and links its reasoning to the material you submit. The evaluation shows where a small local model improves on rules and where it still overreaches. [Try the recorded cases](https://hengyuhang61-creator.github.io/thinkcouncil/)
